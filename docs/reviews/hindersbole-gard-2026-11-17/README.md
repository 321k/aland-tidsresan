# Hindersböle gård, Berggård and the fox farms — 2026-11-17

Five dated places from Nya Åland's article "När Hindersböle blev ”Lilla Finland”" (Wickström and Örjans,
10.12.2009), checked against Mariehamn's orthophotos 1942–2015, the town's address points and property map. They are
added like the demolished house at Tvärgränd 2: a curated record in `dist/assets/historic-buildings.json` and an
entry in `data/object-links.json` with no outline, compiled into a point-only object with a standing and a
demolished state.

| place | point | standing | evidence and uncertainty |
|---|---|---|---|
| Hindersböle gård | address point Blåsippsgränd 3 | 1925–1960 | The article puts the manor house at no. 3 and says it was torn down before 1961. 1925 is the first year in the source, not a construction year (the farm is older). The photos show no house at the address point 1942–1962. A house about 65 m east stands in 1942 and 1952 and is gone in 1962, with a smaller house beside it: a likely candidate, recorded only in the note (`aerials-1942-1975.jpg`, `property-boundaries.png`). |
| Berggård | address point Blomstervägen 20 | 1945–2008 | Hulda Mäki's home during her plot sales 1945–1956; burned 2008. Construction year unknown. Houses stand just west of the address point 1942–1962, but the photos show none at the point in 1975 and 1991, so the site is not verified. |
| Sandås rävfarm | the pens on the 1942 photo | 1939–1951 | Set up before the Second World War; ended 1951 when Verner Söderlund died; the greenhouses on the same property appear in 1975. Pens visible 1942 and 1952. 1939 is an approximate start. |
| Askuddens rävfarm | the pens south of Lemlandsvägen | 1939–1961 | The article places it south of today's Lemlandsvägen. Pens visible 1942 and 1952, only traces in 1962, overgrown by 1975. No end year in the source. |
| Treans rävfarm | the large pen field east of Askuddsvägen | 1939–1951 | Site not documented: the point marks the 1942 pen field between Sandås and Askudden, whose pens are gone in 1952. Flagged as uncertain in the precision and note. |

Images: `fox-farms-1942.jpg`, `fox-farms-1952.jpg` (the area with today's streets), `sites-1942-2015.jpg` (each point on
the photos 1942–2015). The photo year is evidence, not a construction year.

Scope: against the catalogue before these places, exactly the five objects added and nothing else changed (checked
in memory, then in the full build with Norrböle and the street review). Tests: `tests/history.test.cjs` (count and
the five records), `tests/temporal-objects.test.cjs` (point-only objects and their states); 529 of 529 JS tests and the
affected Python tests pass (`logs/`).
